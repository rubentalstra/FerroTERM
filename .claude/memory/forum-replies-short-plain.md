A forum reply drafted for the owner stays near 100 to 150 words and reads like a person typing, with none of the AI-tell words or hedges. A 408-word draft was rejected hard on 2026-09-12, and a 186-word one the same day was rejected as "so much like AI".

**Why:** the owner posts under their own name on the openEHR Discourse and is judged on it. Readers there spot generated text fast.

**How to apply:** no numbered or bulleted points inside a reply, no "Two things I would like to settle" framing, no "reads well to me", no neat parallel sentences ("X does A. Y loses that."), no closing question that surveys the options. Write it as running prose with contractions, one thought per paragraph, and a single plain question at the end if one is needed. Address people by their @handle (the owner wants the mention kept), then continue in plain prose. Every spec or standard the reply leans on gets an inline markdown link to the exact section anchor, checked live before posting, so readers can fact-check (owner rule 2026-09-12).
