A draft the owner will post as themself (discourse, GitHub, mail) stays near
100 to 150 words, one point per paragraph, no recap of what the other person
said, no "working position" summaries. No AI-tell vocabulary at all: delve,
underscore, pivotal, realm, harness, illuminate, facilitate, refine, bolster,
streamline, differentiate, revolutionize, innovative, cutting-edge,
game-changing, transformative, seamless, scalable; no "that being said", "at
its core", "to put it simply", "a key takeaway", "from a broader perspective";
no hedges like "generally speaking", "typically", "tends to", "arguably", "to
some extent", "broadly speaking".

**Why:** the owner called a 408-word reply draft "way and way and way too
long" and said the pattern is forbidden. Their voice on the forum is terse.

**How to apply:** write the draft, then cut it in half. Say the conclusion,
one supporting fact, one open question, sign off. See [[owner-work-style]].
